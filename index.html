How much does a website cost?

Landing pages start at $150, business websites at $300, online stores at $600. The final price depends on pages, features and content, and you get it as a fixed quote before I start.

Do I need to write the text and take the photos?

It helps if you send your own, and I will tell you exactly what I need. If you do not have them yet, we can plan them together.

Can I pay in birr or by bank transfer?

Yes, quotes can be agreed in birr or dollars, and I work with bank transfer.

Who owns the finished site?

You do. The domain and hosting are set up in your name, and you get the code.

What happens after launch?

The site keeps running. If you want changes later, I quote them upfront.

I use AI tools to move faster, and I read and test everything before it ships.
